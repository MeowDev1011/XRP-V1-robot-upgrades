⚡ What is this?

The XRP (Experiential Robotics Platform, by SparkFun · WPI · DEKA · FIRST) is a Pico W powered robot with encoders, an IMU, an ultrasonic sensor and servos. This repository holds the master software our team runs during matches: a continuous 50 Hz console driven by a real gamepad, wrapped by six assist weapons — five specialists plus an on-board brain — that correct, boost, think and protect the pilot.

⚔️ The 6 Secret Weapons

One weapon at a time builds, one match at a time improves. All weapons are continuous, sensor-driven assistance — no one-button macros, no blocking waits, everything runs on the board.

🎯 Gyro-Lock PID

A real-time PID (Kp, Ki, Kd) on imu.get_yaw() that locks the heading whenever you drive straight and instantly kills spins, drifts and collisions.

  • File: weapon1_gyro_lock.py
  • Tune: Kp=0.064 · Ki=0.06 · Kd=0.0055 · MIN_EFFORT=0.14

💪 Torque Boost

Compares desired vs encoder-measured speed. Detects stalls at low speed and pours in extra torque, then releases it the instant the wheels flow again.

  • File: weapon2_torque_boost.py
  • Tune: STALL_RATIO=0.4 · BOOST_MAX=0.35 · BOOST_STEP=0.012

🕹️ Cubic Curves

Cubic response curves turn raw stick input into surgical millimetre moves while keeping 100% top speed for long runs.

  • File: weapon3_curves.py
  • Tune: MIX=0.35 · MIX_PRECISION=0.12

🛞 Traction & Brake

Ramps launches so wheels never slip from zero, and applies a controlled inverse pulse through the encoders when the command returns to zero — the robot nails its position.

  • File: weapon4_traction.py
  • Tune: RAMP_MAX=3.0 · PULSE_K=0.012 · RPM_STOP_MIN=3.0

⏱️ Auto-Endgame

Counts exactly 150,000 ms, fuses encoders + IMU into an X, Y, θ odometry, and drives the robot back home during the final 12 s so the park scores.

  • File: weapon5_endgame.py
  • Tune: V_RETURN=0.85 · PARK_LOCK_CM=8 · FINAL_SAFE_MS=2000

🧠 The Brain — on-board intelligence

The robot thinks by itself 50 times per second — zero internet, zero API. It detects drift, crashes and stalls, and re-tunes the other weapons live (KP/KD/boost) inside safe clamps, then everything decays back to the proven constants.

  • File: weapon6_brain.py
  • Thoughts: GAIN · DAMP · GRIP · HOME

🧑‍🚀 Pilot console

The real gamepad at 50 Hz is always the boss. Assist modes: STRAIGHT, TURN, RETURN and STOP — piloting built for a young gamer with great instincts, not yet adult thumb precision. The kid never needs to reverse.

  • File: pilot_gamepad.py · main_match.py

🎮 How a match is played

Iron Acres matches are exactly 2 minutes 30 seconds long. Points come from game objects, a goal bonus and parking in the home zone at the end. That is why weapon 5 exists.

🗺️ The Iron Acres field (272 × 137 cm)

🕒 The clock rules

150,000 ms total. At t = 138,000 ms the endgame alert turns on: LED blink + assisted return home. In the last 2,000 ms driving is safe-locked for a guaranteed park.

🧭 Pilot priority

Gamepad sticks are ALWAYS more important than any assist. Weapons only act around the pilot's intent — they never fight the sticks.

📊 The 3 P-metrics

  • P1 — spin right after a crash (> 3°) must recover
  • P2 — cruise heading error must stay under 12°
  • P3 — park must nail inside the zone (rpm < 1.5, < 10 cm)

📥 Install on the robot — 3 ways

🥇 XRPCode (official web editor)

  1. Open xrpcode.wpi.edu in Chrome, Edge or Opera.
  2. Plug the XRP by USB and click Connect (WebSerial).
  3. Create files in the filesystem panel and paste each weaponN_*.py, pilot_gamepad.py, weapon6_brain.py, main_match.py.
  4. In the Python tab run import main_match.

🥈 USB serial (mpremote / Thonny)

🚑 Troubleshooting quick table

🔀 Robot rotates by itself after a hit

Normal compensation window: P1 gives 3° to recover. If it keeps spinning, check the IMU is still flat-mounted and recalibrate on the start pad (START edge resets the yaw target).

🐢 Wheels buzz but the robot doesn't move

Torque boost is fighting a real stall: lift the robot and check the gearbox; on the track verify the battery is above 7.4 V (BATTERY_COMP scales effort down).

🎯 It drifts to one side going straight

Trim deadzone: the pilot's Y/X small offsets re-aim the locked heading. Increase TRIM_SAT following offsets if the kid needs a bigger "nudge range" (calibration guide in the wiki).
